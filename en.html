          Impact, model and plan
          
    
          Impact, Business Model & Pilot
          
    
          How AffordBase changes people's lives, how it sustains itself, and what we will do in the first six months of the pilot.
          
  
          

  
          
    
          
      
          The problem
          
      
            
        
          • Without a credit history and a down payment, a mortgage is out of reach. Young professionals, migrants and the self-employed stay renters.
            • 
        
          • Renting alone eats 40–60% of income. At that level, saving for a home of your own is almost impossible.
            • 
        
          • One month without income can end in eviction, and leases often contain unlawful terms.
            • 
      
          
    
          
    
          
      
          What makes us different
          
      
            
        
          • Not a single service but a path: co-living → track record → protection → ownership.
            • 
        
          • Paying rent and utilities on time becomes an asset: a reliability score.
            • 
        
          • Homes are bought 1 m² at a time, so the first step is affordable on an ordinary salary.
            • 
        
          • The city provides land, investors provide capital at 3–5%, members make regular payments.
            • 
      
          
    
          
  
          

  
          
    
          What we measure
          
    
          
      
          < 30%of income spent on housing by members (today 40–60%)
          
      
          0evictions of fund members due to a temporary loss of income
          
      
          m²owned by members, growing every month
          
      
          % thin-fileof members with no credit history who secured housing
          
    
          
  
          

  
          
    
          
      
          How the platform sustains itself
          
      
          
        
          Pool management
          a fee paid by the co-op for managing the project, procurement and share accounting
          
        
          Bulk procurement
          supplier commission for a guaranteed order volume
          
        
          City dashboard
          an akimat subscription for analytics and tracking of transferred properties
          
        
          Investment placement
          a service fee from investors who fund pools
          
      
          
      
          The 1.5% transaction contribution is not our revenue: all of it goes to the members' mutual-aid fund.
